\title{Momentum Regime Shifts: A Bayesian Hidden Markov Model Approach to Cross-Sectional Stock Selection}
\author{Gilad Gang}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This thesis investigates how a real-time regime signal interacts with cross-sectional momentum. A Bayesian Hidden Markov Model produces a filtered probability of market panic, which is combined with momentum at 12 lookback horizons in machine learning models to rank stocks. Out-of-sample on 2011--2025 U.S. equities, only a nonlinear model (XGBoost) produces meaningful long-short returns; a linear model with identical features collapses. Model-free evidence confirms the mechanism: momentum is not a single anomaly but a family of horizon-specific effects that rotates with market conditions -- continuation in calm, reversal in panic. The regime signal functions as a context variable governing this rotation, complementing the exposure-scaling approach of Daniel and Moskowitz (2016) with a stock-selection channel.
\end{abstract}
